# 💫 About Me:
I am a passionate Computer Science undergraduate at Jaypee University of Engineering and Technology (Expected 2027) with a strong interest in Android development, Artificial Intelligence, and system design. I enjoy building impactful, real-world solutions that combine clean architecture with intelligent automation.<br><br>My technical expertise spans Java, Python, JavaScript, Kotlin, and C++, along with hands-on experience in Android SDK, Supabase, Firebase, OpenCV, Mediapipe, TensorFlow, and RESTful APIs. I have worked on AI-driven systems such as a real-time Driver Monitoring System achieving 95% drowsiness detection accuracy and a Secure Online Voting System implementing encryption, facial recognition, and multi-factor authentication.<br><br>As a Software Development Intern at Consilio Intelligence Research Lab, I gained practical exposure to machine learning workflows, data pipelines, and Agile-based product development. Beyond technical skills, I actively contribute to leadership and campus initiatives as Joint Secretary of JUET Youth Club, where I lead technical events impacting 500+ students.<br><br>I am deeply interested in Data Structures & Algorithms, scalable mobile systems, and AI-powered applications, and I continuously strive to enhance my problem-solving and engineering capabilities.
